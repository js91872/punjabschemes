# Scheme research workflow

The inventory is an editorial research queue, not website content. An entry must not be copied into `src/lib/schemes.ts` until its current rules are confirmed from the responsible department.

## Verification gate

For every scheme selected for publication:

1. Confirm that applications or benefits remain operational.
2. Capture the responsible department and the most specific official source URL.
3. Verify benefit amounts, eligibility, exclusions, documents, application channel and deadlines.
4. Record the source publication/update date when available and the date reviewed.
5. Resolve renamed, overlapping or centrally sponsored variants to avoid duplicate pages.
6. Draft in plain language without copying government text.
7. Add an explicit official-source link and independent-site disclaimer.

## Initial launch shortlist

The `launch` priority in `scheme-inventory.json` is provisional. It identifies high-utility scheme families for detailed verification, not a claim that every scheme is currently open.
